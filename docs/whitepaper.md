# Ryne Whitepaper

## Vision

Market noise on Solana is loud, fast and scattered across a dozen tabs. Ryne imagines a single board that stays calm while everything around it shouts: ten coins, ranked by what actually traded, readable in a glance.

## Mission

Turn a raw public feed into something a person can trust at a glance. Show real numbers, show them honestly when the source fails, and never invent a fact that has not been stated.

## Principles

- **One record per outside fact.** An unstated fact renders inert, with no value and no link.
- **Real data only.** Figures come straight from a public market source and are never padded.
- **Quiet by default.** Motion informs, never decorates, and every animation has an instant fallback.
